# Needle 0.6.1

## What's new

- **Discover recordings beyond Spotify:** sync your top 20 artists, choose up to five, and explore popular uploads from Needle’s sources. Results show reported plays/views and available bitrate. Send a recording to Review Queue to preview and approve it before downloading.
- **Add your collection to Spotify:** Library’s **Add all to Spotify** queues eligible saved MP3s for your configured playlist, skipping recordings already added or waiting.
- **Clearer, more reliable delivery:** temporary Spotify local-file errors no longer stop the companion. Needle checks album metadata to distinguish otherwise matching recordings and shows specific indexing or matching problems.
- **Simpler companion setup:** enable or update Needle’s bundled [Spicetify](https://spicetify.app/) companion from the in-app setup guide. Needle quits and reopens Spotify after applying the companion, so the updated integration becomes active immediately.

- **Retry uncertain catalog checks:** failed checks explicitly show that availability is unknown. Retry an individual recording without searching for uploads again; successful matches can open directly in Spotify.

## Install

For **Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or newer), macOS 15 Sequoia or later**. Download **Needle-0.6.1.dmg**, open it, and drag Needle to Applications.

This app is unsigned and not notarized. If macOS blocks launch, choose **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. See [Getting started](https://github.com/icebli/needle/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md). Audio tools are included; no Homebrew, Rust, or Python setup is needed.

## Upgrade

Choose **Needle → Check for Updates…**. Updates are authenticated with Needle’s update-signing key. macOS may require Open Anyway again after an unsigned update. Manual replacement using the DMG also works. Saved music, history, settings, and the review queue stay on your computer.

After upgrading, use **Enable / update Needle companion** in the Spotify setup guide to refresh the Spotify integration. Install Spicetify first if needed.

## Spotify and discovery

Spotify Local Files works without Spicetify. Automatic playlist delivery and artist discovery require Spotify desktop, [Spicetify](https://spicetify.app/), and Needle’s companion. Enable Local Files and add your Needle music folder as a Spotify source.

Top artists reflect roughly six months of Spotify affinity, rather than exact play counts. Discovery ranks reported source plays/views among the returned search results. “No exact catalog match” is a lead to review, not proof a recording is absent from Spotify everywhere. Regional availability, alternate titles, and versions can affect matching. Spotify updates may require refreshing Spicetify.

Needle never downloads discovery results without approval. Missing bitrate or play counts remain Unknown. The installer is built on an Apple Silicon macOS 15 runner; testing on a separate clean macOS 15 machine remains outstanding.

Download **needle-companion.zip** for Chrome, Firefox, or Zen and follow the [browser companion guide](https://github.com/icebli/needle/blob/main/docs/browser-extension.md).
