# Needle 0.8.1

## What changed

The recording workspace now places large artwork and recording identity above compact source rows. A dedicated right inspector scrolls independently, with metadata review and bookmark actions pinned at the bottom. Queue selection moves into a compact picker, and playback stays in one continuous bottom strip.

This release also removes promotional copy and applies native Liquid Glass to search and playback controls on macOS 26 and later. Exact-source approval, rejection warnings, and explicit metadata review remain in place.

## Install

For Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or newer), macOS 15 Sequoia or later. Download **Needle-0.8.1.dmg**, open it, and drag Needle to Applications. Audio tools are included; Homebrew, Rust, and Python are not required.

The app is unsigned and not notarized. If macOS blocks launch, use **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. See [Getting started](https://github.com/icebli/needle/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md).

## Upgrade

Choose **Needle → Check for Updates…** after the update feed is published, or replace the app manually with the DMG. Updates are authenticated with Needle’s update-signing key. macOS may require Open Anyway again. Your saved music, settings, history, bookmarks, and review queue remain on your computer.

Spotify Local Files works without Spicetify. Automatic playlist delivery and artist discovery require Spotify desktop, Spicetify, and Needle’s companion. Use **Enable / update Needle companion** in the setup guide to refresh that integration.

Download **needle-companion.zip** for Chrome, Firefox, or Zen and follow the [browser companion guide](https://github.com/icebli/needle/blob/main/docs/browser-extension.md).

## Verification and limits

Source and UI checks cover exact-source approval, excluded recordings, source switching, metadata review, gallery navigation, and live playback controls. The release workflow also tests the bundled tools without Homebrew on PATH and validates corresponding-source materials. A separate clean macOS 15 machine has not been tested. Source bitrate and popularity remain provider reports; Needle does not claim fingerprint or mastering verification.
